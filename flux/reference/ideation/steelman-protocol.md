# Steelman Delta

Purpose: Flux `steelman` sequence, neutrality, and handoff contract. General steelmanning theory is model-known.

## Five Phases

1. `RECEIVE`: restate the idea and asymmetric stakes; confirm material ambiguity before continuing.
2. `FOR`: present the strongest mechanism-backed proponent case without counterarguments.
3. `AGAINST`: present the strongest specific failure case and best alternative without rebuttal.
4. `SYNTHESIZE`: identify collisions, talking-past points, and the one decisive question.
5. `SOFT VERDICT`: state what must be true for each side and the cheapest discriminating experiment. Flux does not issue the final Go/No-Go.

Each side must be recognizable to a thoughtful advocate of that side. Rebuild a weak side before synthesis; do not manufacture symmetry when a legal, ethical, or technical blocker is decisive.

## Summary Contract

Include:

- idea and stakes if right/wrong
- strongest case for, with mechanisms and evidence
- strongest case against, with failure conditions and alternative
- decisive question
- conditions for each side to win
- cheapest time-boxed experiment
- unresolved evidence and suggested next step

## Routing

- Formal decision -> Magi.
- Positive case proceeds -> Spark or Scribe[unified].
- Negative case implies subtraction -> Void.
- Experiment pending -> return to Flux after results.
