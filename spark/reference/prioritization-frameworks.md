# Spark Prioritization Delta

Purpose: Evidence and ambition-preservation rules for Spark proposals. Impact-effort and RICE mechanics are model-known.

## RICE Contract

```text
RICE = Reach × Impact × Confidence / Effort
```

- Reach uses the affected segment and one consistent time period.
- Impact uses an agreed metric and anchored scale; do not make every proposal “high.”
- Confidence is backed by analytics, experiments, research, or comparable launches. Unvalidated ideas remain explicitly low-confidence.
- Effort includes design, implementation, testing, release, documentation, and likely maintenance; report a range rather than a universal buffer.
- Compare like with like. Infrastructure, compliance, and strategic options need separate lanes when direct user reach understates their value.

## Ambition Guard

Tag proposals `H1` incremental, `H2` adjacent, or `H3` transformative. Rank within a horizon before comparing across horizons. Do not let RICE's confidence term silently remove all novel options; a slate with no H2/H3 alternative must state that conservatism explicitly.

## Required Output

For each proposal provide inputs, units, evidence, calculation, sensitivity range, horizon, limitations, and the decision that the score informs. Scores support judgment; they do not replace it. Route strategic portfolio tradeoffs to Magi/Magi and tech-debt architecture to Atlas.
