# Kano Survey Delta

Purpose: Voice instrument fidelity and interpretation contract. General Kano theory is model-known.

## Instrument

Ask paired questions for the same feature:

- Functional: how do you feel if the feature is present?
- Dysfunctional: how do you feel if the feature is absent?

Use the canonical responses consistently: `Like`, `Expect`, `Neutral`, `Live with`, `Dislike`. Rewording requires re-validation; do not mix a changed instrument with canonical classification tables.

## Classification Matrix

| Functional \\ Dysfunctional | Like | Expect | Neutral | Live with | Dislike |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Like | Q | D | D | D | P |
| Expect | R | I | I | I | M |
| Neutral | R | I | I | I | M |
| Live with | R | I | I | I | M |
| Dislike | R | R | R | R | Q |

`M` Must-have, `P` Performance, `D` Delighter, `I` Indifferent, `R` Reverse, `Q` Questionable.

## Reporting Contract

- Report counts and uncertainty per relevant segment; do not use universal sample-size cutoffs as proof of stability.
- Keep R and Q visible and investigate wording or segment conflict.
- Do not mix bug fixes with optional feature concepts in one interpretation.
- State field date; Kano categories can change as expectations mature.

Route Must-have/Delighter findings to Spark, performance measurement to Pulse, Q-heavy wording to Field, and persona divergence to Echo.
